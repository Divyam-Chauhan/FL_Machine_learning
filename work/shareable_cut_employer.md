# Shareable cut: employer three-sentence summary

I built a warehouse-based content opportunity ranking with March-only features, a transparent ML-07 baseline, and five client-grouped validation folds over 39,545 eligible candidates.

Within-fold macro Precision@50 was 0.804 for L2 Logistic Regression versus 0.420 for the rule, with the model higher in all five folds; fold-macro ROC-AUC was 0.636 vs. 0.438 and average precision was 0.655 vs. 0.501.

These are retrospective results from one adjacent-month cohort and support human review only; they do not establish future-cohort performance, page staleness, causal refresh impact, or traffic recovery. [Read the case study](https://divyam-chauhan.github.io/FL_Machine_learning/).
