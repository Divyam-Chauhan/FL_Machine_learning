# Shareable cut: employer three-sentence summary

I built a content-opportunity review ranking from the gated FlyRank warehouse, comparing March-only L2 Logistic Regression with the ML-07 March-impressions rule on 39,545 eligible page-client items.
Within-fold macro P@50 was 0.804 vs. 0.420, with the model higher in all five client-grouped folds; macro ROC-AUC was 0.636 vs. 0.438 and average precision was 0.655 vs. 0.501.

These results describe one retrospective adjacent-month cohort and support human review only; they do not establish future-cohort performance, page staleness, causal refresh impact, or traffic recovery ([case study](https://divyam-chauhan.github.io/FL_Machine_learning/)).
